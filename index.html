How I work

A repeatable pipeline. The surface decides what gets tested — not a preconceived class.

P·1

Recon that never closes

Passive discovery, JS bundles, mobile artifacts, and a target's own open source. Every new surface goes back on the map and re-derives what's worth testing.

gate — the map explains every test
P·2

Coverage, then focused depth

Cover the breadth first; then chase one anomaly at a time to a candidate or a documented refutation. Depth never blocks breadth.

gate — every anomaly resolved
P·3

Materialize the impact

A mechanism isn't a finding. The consequence is crossed on accounts I own, captured live, with positive and negative controls — re-verified at the moment of report.

gate — impact crossed, not asserted
P·4

Reproduce blind, then triage hostile

The steps are run from scratch by someone who wasn't there, then reviewed adversarially — before anything is written up. It reproduces on the first try, or it isn't ready.

gate — survives a skeptic
P·5

Report for the decision

Consequence over mechanism, the evidence where the doubt is, severity charged to what a byte supports. Coordinated disclosure, secrets masked, conduct declared.

gate — no point left to believe on faith

Principles

The discipline that makes a finding a paid, repeatable relationship.

01 Authorized only

Public and private bug-bounty programs, CTFs, labs, and systems I own. Scope and rules are read before a single request leaves my machine.

02 Coordinated disclosure

Details stay private until fixed and permitted. Only sanitized methodology is ever published.

03 Real impact, no inflation

I charge the highest severity the evidence supports — and not a point more. Unproven ceilings stay out of the report.

04 Nothing hazardous

No brute force, enumeration, denial of service, or touching another person's data. Two accounts I own prove cross-account access.
